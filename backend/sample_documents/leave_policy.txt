COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave

Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 3 working days before the requested leave date.

Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per year.

For sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive working days, employees must provide a medical certificate to HR.

Emergency Leave

Employees may request emergency leave in urgent personal or family situations.

Emergency leave should be communicated to the employee's manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Leave Approval

Leave requests must be submitted through the company's internal employee portal.

The employee's reporting manager is responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward for a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year.
